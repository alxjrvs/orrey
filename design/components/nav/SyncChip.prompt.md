One-line: states whether Discord and Google Calendar are current, in the top bar's right-hand cells.

```jsx
<SyncChip status="ok" label="Discord OK" />
<SyncChip status="stale" label="GCal 4m ago" />
<SyncChip status="off" label="Rowan" />
```

Square 5px dot, no ring, no animation. Chips are separated by a left hairline and fill the full bar height — they are cells, not floating badges.
