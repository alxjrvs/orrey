What the Orrey knows about you

Last updated 13 September 2026.

The Orrey schedules games for one Discord server. It is run by the server's own organiser, for that server, and for nothing else. There is no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

What it stores

  • Your Discord user id. This is how Orrey knows who you are. It is the only identity Orrey uses.
  • Your username and display name, cached so posts can name you without asking Discord every time. Orrey treats these as a copy, never as the truth.
  • What you said about sessions — in, out, maybe, any note you attached, and afterwards whether you came.
  • A calendar feed token, a random string that lets a calendar app subscribe to your games. Anyone holding it can read that feed, which is why it is not shown publicly.
  • Whether Orrey can DM you. Discord only reveals this by refusing, so Orrey records the refusal and uses the channel instead.

What it does not store

  • No message content, beyond what you type into Orrey's own buttons and forms.
  • No email address, no password. Sign-in is Discord's identify scope alone — Orrey never asks Discord for the list of servers you are in.
  • No payment details. Orrey takes no money.

Where it lives

In a Cloudflare D1 database owned by the server's organiser. Orrey's database is the source of truth for scheduling: what you see in Discord and in Google Calendar are copies Orrey writes outward. Orrey writes to its own Google calendar only, never to a personal one.

Deleting it

Run /console in the server and choose Delete my data. It asks once to confirm, then removes your row and everything keyed to your Discord id, and tells you what it removed. Nothing is retained afterwards, and your calendar feed token stops working immediately.

Deleting your data does not remove you from the Discord server, and it does not undo posts Orrey already sent — those are ordinary Discord messages and can be deleted by a moderator. If you use Orrey again afterwards, it starts over knowing nothing.

Asking a person

Orrey is small enough that the answer to anything not covered here is: ask the server's organiser. They run it and hold the database.
